# Operations VA — Run a Pre-Built Facebook Ads System for a Real Estate Agency

**The job in one sentence:**
We've built a fully automated agency operations system ("AgencyOS") that runs a real-estate Facebook ads agency end-to-end. The system tells you — step by step, in Slack — exactly what to do, when, and how. Your job is to execute it with care.

This is a **craft role**, and the craft is *precision*. There is a written SOP for every task. There are no judgment calls, no improvisation, no ambiguity — every action you take has a documented step and a documented next step. Good operators take pride in running a clean process. If that's you, you'll fit in here.

The thinking layer is already built. Strategy, decisions, optimization, and creative direction are handled by AI agents and the founder. What we need is a steady hand on the wheel.

---

## What you'll actually be doing (concretely)

Each task below has a written SOP and Slack tells you when it's your turn:

- Inviting new clients to their dedicated Slack channel as single-channel guests
- Requesting Meta Business Portfolio access from new clients in Meta Business Manager
- Setting up the Lead Centre and confirming leads flow into the client's CRM (we'll show you exactly how — typically GoHighLevel or a Google Sheet)
- Editing client-recorded videos (basic cuts, captions, brand frame — not motion graphics)
- Building Facebook campaigns in Ads Manager following our naming and structure SOP
- Running a 9-point QA checklist before any campaign goes live (every item is in the checklist — you tick, you don't decide)
- Publishing campaigns and flipping our project tracker to Live
- Executing optimization tasks the AI agent assigns — swap creative, adjust audience, cut/pause campaign — with the exact spec given to you
- Logging blockers when something can't be completed so the founder is alerted

You are **not** writing strategy, scripts, or creative copy. You are not making targeting calls or budget decisions. Those are handled.

---

## You'll thrive here if you:

- Read SOPs carefully and assume every step is there for a reason
- Don't skip steps when you're tired or in a hurry
- Communicate the moment something looks off, instead of guessing
- Are calm, low-drama, and reliable
- Have stable internet, a quiet workspace, and a backup power plan
- Are comfortable in Slack, Google Workspace, Facebook Ads Manager, and standard web apps (no coding required, but Ads Manager experience is a major plus)
- Write and read clear English

## This probably isn't your fit if you:

- Skip steps when you're tired or confident you "get it"
- Quietly improvise instead of flagging when something looks off
- Are juggling several clients and can't actively watch Slack during your shift
- Need to be told the same thing twice
