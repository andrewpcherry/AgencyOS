
SOP 06
Tasks
Your single work queue. Open My Queue. Work top to bottom. Repeat.

TL;DR — In 30 Seconds
Your Daily Workflow
Open SmartSuite → Tasks app → My Queue view.
Queue is sorted by Priority (Urgent → High → Normal → Low), then Due Date.
Start at the top. Open the first task.
Read: task name, linked client, notes.
Do the work.
Update Status = Done when complete.
Move to the next task.
That's it. Top to bottom. Don't skip tasks without a reason.

The 15 Task Types
These are the categories of work you'll see in your queue. Each tells you what kind of work is needed.

How to Work a Task
Open My Queue.
Click the top task to open it.
Read everything:
Task — what you're doing.
Client — click through to see their details.
Campaign — if applicable, which campaign this relates to.
Type — confirms the kind of work.
Priority — Urgent means now.
Due Date — when it's due.
Notes — specific instructions or context. Always read.
Do the work (Ads Manager, Campaigns app, wherever applies).
When done: change Status → Done.
If you can't complete it: Status → Blocked + fill in Blocker Notes.
Move to the next task in the queue.

If You're Blocked
A task is Blocked when you cannot complete it due to something outside your control.
Change Status → Blocked.
Fill in Blocker Notes — be specific so Andrew can unblock.
Post a /update Tasks message in #team-<client> with Status: Blocked. Don't just quietly mark it blocked and move on.
Common blockers:
Client hasn't given access yet.
Client hasn't sent videos yet.
A field in the Campaign record is missing (e.g., no Asset Link).
Ad account has a policy issue.
Once the blocker is resolved, n8n or Andrew will unblock the task and it returns to your queue.

What NOT to Do
Don't create tasks manually. n8n creates tasks. If you think a task is missing, tell Andrew.
Don't mark a task Done if you haven't actually completed the work. Be honest.
Don't ignore blocked tasks. Flag them in Slack and keep checking back.
Don't work on tasks assigned to someone else without being asked.
Don't skip Urgent tasks to work on easier Normal tasks. Work priority order.
Don't delete tasks. Even if a task seems wrong, mark it Done and flag to Andrew.

Priority | What it means | Response time
Urgent | Do this before anything else. Time-sensitive or blocking the whole client. | Within 1–2 hours
High | Important. Do today. | Same day
Normal | Standard work. Do within 24–48 hours. | 1–2 days
Low | Nice-to-do. Work it when the queue is clear. | 3–5 days
Status | What it means
To Do | Task is in your queue, ready to work.
In Progress | You've started this task.
Blocked | You can't complete this due to an external blocker.
Done | Task is complete.
 | Don't leave In Progress
Don't leave tasks as In Progress for more than 24 hours without an update. If you're still working on something, add a note + message Andrew on Slack.

Problem | What to do
My Queue is empty but I know there's work to do | Check the Onboarding app — there may be steps awaiting completion. Tell Andrew.
A task is assigned to me but I don't know what it means | Read Notes and the linked Campaign / Client record. Still unclear? Ask Andrew.
A task was created for the wrong client | Flag to Andrew. Don't move it yourself.
I completed work but n8n didn't create the next task | Tell Andrew — automation may have failed.
I see tasks from other clients in My Queue | Check the Assigned To filter on the view. Should only show your tasks.
