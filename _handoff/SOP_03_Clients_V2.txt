How Andrew Updates a Client Record
Open the client record.
Update the relevant field as the client completes their setup (e.g., Ad Account ID).
If their phase changes (Onboarding → Active), update Status.
Add important context to Notes.

What NOT to Do
Don't create client records manually. Win the deal in Pipeline → M5 creates the record.
Don't delete client records. Even churned clients stay as historical data.
Don't edit Slack Channel ID unless Andrew has given you the correct ID.
VAs: don't change the Status field. Only Andrew manages status.
Don't store video scripts or creative assets in this app. Those go in Google Drive — link via Drive Folder URL.

 | The big idea
Clients is the source of truth for who a client is — contact details, Meta account IDs, status, links to everywhere they live in the system.
You don't track onboarding here (Onboarding app). You don't build campaigns here (Campaigns app). You don't manage tasks here (Tasks app). This is just the directory.
 | Records are auto-created
When a deal hits Won in Pipeline, the M5 Deal Won workflow (live since April 24, 2026) creates the Client record + Onboarding record + Drive folder + Slack channels automatically. Don't create them manually.
Status | What it means
Onboarding | Client is new. Onboarding steps in progress. Campaigns not yet live.
Active | Campaigns are live. Client is in the monthly retainer phase.
Paused | Campaigns paused (client travelling, budget pause).
Churned | Client has left. Keep the record — don't delete.
 | The Won flow (M5 — auto)
1. Andrew sets Pipeline deal_stage = Won.
2. M5 webhook fires within seconds.
3. Client record created here with basic info pulled from the deal.
4. Onboarding record created in Onboarding app, linked to this Client.
5. Drive folder created under RequityAI / [Client Name].
6. Slack channels (#team-[client] private, #client-[client] public) created.
7. Niche-aware video brief delivered based on Client.Niche field.
8. Confirmation posted in #team-operations.
 | VAs: do not edit fields here
Unless Andrew specifically tells you to. Particularly: never change Status, Slack Channel ID, or any of the account ID fields. If something looks wrong, flag it.
Field | What it's for
Business Name | Primary identifier. Use their actual business name.
Status | Onboarding / Active / Paused / Churned. Always keep current.
Niche | REI (real estate investor wholesaler) / Notes (notes business) / Other. Drives niche-aware video brief delivery (M5). Ritsel Notes is Notes, not REI.
Market / Region | Geographic target market (e.g., "Phoenix, AZ", "Dallas-Fort Worth").
Ad Account ID | Facebook Ad Account ID. Find in Meta Business Manager. Numbers only.
Business Portfolio ID | Meta Business Portfolio ID. Needed for VA to request access.
Facebook Page URL | Full URL to their Facebook business page.
Drive Folder URL | Link to their Google Drive folder with scripts and creative assets.
Slack Channel ID | Used by n8n to route notifications to the right Slack channel. Andrew sets this.
Assigned VA | Which VA handles this client's campaigns.
Link to Onboarding | Connects to the Onboarding app record. Auto-created by M5.
Link to Campaigns | Connects to all Campaign records for this client.
Live Campaigns | Auto-counted from linked campaigns. Shows how many are running.
Open Tasks | Auto-counted from linked tasks. Shows pending VA work.
View | When to use it
Dashboard | Main view. All clients with status, live campaigns, open tasks at a glance.
Client Onboarding Form | The form new clients fill out. Don't edit this view.
Problem | What to do
Client record wasn't created after a deal was Won | Check #team-operations for an error. M5 may have failed — Andrew will reconcile.
Ad Account ID is blank | Client hasn't shared it yet. Check Onboarding app for the step status.
Client shows wrong status | Flag to Andrew — only Andrew changes Status.
Drive Folder URL is empty | Drive folder hasn't been created yet (BegEckXSgXmVffX2 runs daily 8am ET). Check with Andrew if it's been more than a day.
Niche is wrong | Ritsel = Notes, not REI. Wrong niche = wrong video brief delivered. Tell Andrew to fix.
